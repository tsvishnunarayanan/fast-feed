About this app

Fast Feed brings useful Vidya ERP tasks into a cleaner Android experience. It helps students automate feedback, view synced marks, check attendance and receive useful alerts without repeatedly navigating the full ERP interface.

  • bolt Feedback automation with submission verification.
  • table_chart Marks archive with semester-wise synced reports.
  • fact_check Overall and subject-wise attendance details.
  • system_update_alt Smaller in-app delta updates when available.

What's new

  • Improved in-app update delivery
